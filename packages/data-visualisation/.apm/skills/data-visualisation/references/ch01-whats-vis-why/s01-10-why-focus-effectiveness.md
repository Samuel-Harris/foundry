# 1.10 Why Focus on Effectiveness?

## Principle

<!-- verbatim p.36 -->
> The focus on effectiveness is a corollary of defining vis to have the goal of supporting user tasks.
<!-- /verbatim -->

This goal puts correctness, accuracy and truth at the centre of vis [p.36].

## Vis versus other image-making fields

Art emphasizes emotion, beauty or provoking thought; movies and comics emphasize narrative; advertising emphasizes mood or selling. For those goals, deliberate distortion and even fabrication of facts is often appropriate [p.36]. A vis designer does not typically have artistic license [p.37].

<!-- verbatim p.37 -->
> Moreover, the phrase "it's not just about making pretty pictures" is a common and vehement assertion in vis, meaning that the goals of the designer are not met if the result is beautiful but not effective.
<!-- /verbatim -->

## Every depiction is an abstraction

No picture can communicate the whole truth and nothing but the truth. Correctness is complicated because any depiction of data is an abstraction, with choices about which aspects to emphasize [p.37]. Cartographers have long experience with the difference between a map and its terrain, and even a photograph involves choices of abstraction and emphasis, such as what to include in the frame [p.37].
