# 1.15 Further Reading

Each Further Reading section suggests further reading on the chapter's ideas and acknowledges key sources that influenced it [p.43].

## Sources by topic

- **Why use an external representation?** Papers on the nature of external representations in problem solving [Zhang 97] and a representational analysis of number systems [Zhang and Norman 95]; Larkin and Simon's *Why A Diagram Is (Sometimes) Worth Ten Thousand Words* is the basis for the chapter's discussion of diagrams [pp.43-44].
- **Why show the data in detail?** Anscombe's paper proposing the quartet [Anscombe 73], and an early paper on the many faces of the scatterplot that discusses why to show as much data as possible [Cleveland and McGill 84b] [p.44].
- **What is the vis design space?** The discussion is based on a paper on design study methodology, covering progress from a loose to a crisp understanding of user requirements [Sedlmair et al. 12] [p.44].
- **What resource limitations matter?** Ware's textbook on human limits in perception, memory and cognition [Ware 13]; a survey of change blindness [Simons 00]; information density traced to Bertin's graphic density [Bertin 67] and Tufte's data–ink ratio [Tufte 83] [p.44].
