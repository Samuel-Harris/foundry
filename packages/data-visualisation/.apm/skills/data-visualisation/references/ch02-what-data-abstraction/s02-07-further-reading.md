# 2.7 Further Reading

## Sources by topic

- **The big picture.** The framework was inspired in part by earlier data taxonomies: the synthesis chapter of an early infovis reader [Card et al. 99], a taxonomy emphasising continuous versus discrete data [Tory and Möller 04a], and one emphasising both data and tasks [Shneiderman 96] [p.65].
- **Field datasets.** Two textbooks [Telea 07, Ward et al. 10], a handbook [Hansen and Johnson 05] and the vtk book [Schroeder et al. 06] [p.65].
- **Attribute types.** Categorical, ordered and quantitative come from the work on scales of measurement in psychophysics [Stevens 46]; scales of measurement are also discussed in The Grammar of Graphics [Wilkinson 05] and form the axes of a vis design space taxonomy [Card and Mackinlay 97] [p.65].
- **Key and value semantics.** The Polaris system, commercialised as Tableau, is built around key attributes (independent dimensions) versus value attributes (dependent measures) [Stolte et al. 02] [p.65].
- **Temporal semantics.** Visualization of Time-Oriented Data [Aigner et al. 11] [p.65].
