# 3.8 Further Reading

## Sources by topic

- **The big picture.** An earlier version of the what–why–how framework appeared as a paper [Brehmer and Munzner 13], with a detailed discussion of its relation to 30 previous classifications and 20 relevant references, from the scientific data analysis process [Springmeyer et al. 92] and an influential low-level task classification [Amar et al. 05] to a taxonomy of network tasks [Lee et al. 06] and a taxonomy of interaction dynamics [Heer and Shneiderman 12] [p.89].
- **Who: designers versus users.** An influential paper on the challenges of bridging the gaps between vis designers and users [van Wijk 06] [p.90].
- **Derive.** Vis pipeline models treat data transformation as a critical early step [Card et al. 99, Chi and Riedl 98]; others point out the need to transform between attribute types [Velleman and Wilkinson 93]; a later taxonomy explicitly discusses data types changing as the result of transformation [Tory and Möller 04b] [p.90].
- **Examples.** SpaceTree [Plaisant et al. 02], TreeJuxtaposer [Munzner et al. 03], Strahler numbers for tree simplification [Auber 02] and linked derived spaces for feature detection [Henze 98] [p.90].
