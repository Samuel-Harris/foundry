# 4.2 Why Validate?

Validation matters for the reasons given in Chapter 1: the vis design space is huge and most designs are ineffective. Chapter 1 also explains why validation is tricky and difficult to get right [p.92].

<!-- verbatim p.92 -->
> It's valuable to think about how you might validate your choices from the very beginning of the design process, rather than leaving these considerations for the end as an afterthought.
<!-- /verbatim -->

The chapter adds two levels to the book's why–what–how analysis: one above the abstraction level and one below the idiom level. The book concentrates on the two middle levels, but considering all four helps when thinking about how to validate whether a design has succeeded [p.92].
