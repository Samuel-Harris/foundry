# 6.2 Why and When to Follow Rules of Thumb?

## What the rules are

<!-- verbatim p.142 -->
> These rules of thumb are my current attempt to synthesize the current state of knowledge into a more unified whole. In some cases I refer to empirical studies, in others I make arguments based on my own experience, and some have been proposed in previous work.
<!-- /verbatim -->

## How settled they are

<!-- verbatim p.142 -->
> They are not set in stone; indeed, they are deeply incomplete. The characterization of what idioms are appropriate for which task and data abstractions is still an ongoing research frontier, and there are many open questions.
<!-- /verbatim -->

The section gives the rules' sources but states no general criterion for when a rule may be set aside [p.142]. Some rules state their own conditions; No Unjustified 3D, for example, names shape understanding of inherently three-dimensional structures as the case where 3D is easy to justify [pp.142-143].
