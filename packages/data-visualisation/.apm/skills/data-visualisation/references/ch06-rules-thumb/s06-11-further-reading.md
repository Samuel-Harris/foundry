# 6.11 Further Reading

## Sources by rule

- **No Unjustified 3D.** Planar versus depth perception and 3D depth cues: both of Ware's books [Ware 08, Ware 13]. An in-depth treatment of 2D versus 3D with references to human factors and air traffic control studies, including Wickens's work [St. John et al. 01]. Experiments that overturned earlier claims of 3D benefits [Cockburn and McKenzie 00, 01, 04] [p.166].
- **Memory.** Memory and attention in vis, in much more detail [Ware 13]; supporting and exploiting spatial memory in user interfaces [Scarr et al. 13] [p.166].
- **Animation.** Choreography to guide the viewer's eyes in narrative storytelling [Lasseter 87]. A meta-review arguing that many seemingly promising animation results are confounded by comparisons of incommensurate situations, and finding small multiples better than animation when equivalent information is shown [Tversky et al. 02] and segmentation is carefully chosen [Zacks and Tversky 03]. A study finding trend animation fast and enjoyable for presentation but error-prone, and significantly slower than small multiples and trace lines for exploratory analysis [Robertson et al. 08] [pp.166-167].
- **Change Blindness.** A survey paper as a starting point [Simons 00] [p.167].
- **Overview, Zoom and Filter, Details on Demand.** The original, very readable paper [Shneiderman 96]; a synthesis review of how overviews are used in infovis [Hornbæk and Hertzum 11] [p.167].
- **Responsiveness Is Required.** Latency classes [Card et al. 91]; an accessible book chapter on them [Johnson 10, Chapter 12]; the costs of interaction [Lam 08] and a framework for interaction [Yi et al. 07] [p.167].
- **Get It Right in Black and White.** Stone's blog post [Stone 10] [p.167].
- **Function First, Form Next.** The Non-Designer's Design Book for basic graphic design guidelines [Williams 08] [p.167].
