# 7.2 Why Arrange?

## Arrangement is the most crucial encoding choice

<!-- verbatim p.170 -->
> The arrange design choice covers all aspects of the use of spatial channels for visual encoding. It is the most crucial visual encoding choice because the use of space dominates the user's mental model of the dataset.
<!-- /verbatim -->

- **Ordered and quantitative attributes.** The three highest-ranked effectiveness channels for them all relate to spatial position: planar position against a common scale, planar position along an unaligned scale, and length [p.170].
- **Categorical attributes.** Their highest-ranked channel, grouping items within the same region, is also about the use of space [p.170].
- **No all-purpose nonspatial channel.** No nonspatial channel is highly effective for all attribute types; the others suit either ordered or categorical attributes but not both, because of the principle of expressiveness [p.170].

The primacy of the spatial position channels, and the principles of effectiveness and expressiveness, are discussed at length in Chapter 5 [p.170].
