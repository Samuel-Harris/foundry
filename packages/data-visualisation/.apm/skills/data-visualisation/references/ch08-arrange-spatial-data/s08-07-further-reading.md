# 8.7 Further Reading

## Sources by topic

- **History.** Thematic cartography, overlaying statistical data on geographic maps, blossomed in the 19th century, introducing choropleth maps, dot maps and proportional symbol maps; its history is documented at <http://www.datavis.ca/milestones> [Friendly 08] [p.222].
- **Cartography.** A scholarly but accessible historical review of thematic cartography structured around marks and channels [MacEachren 79]; MacEachren's book on cartographic representation, visualization and design with respect to cognition and semiotics [MacEachren 95]; and Slocum's textbook as a general reference for the vis audience [Slocum et al. 08] [p.222].
- **Spatial fields.** An overview chapter on spatial field encoding and interaction idioms [Schroeder and Martin 05], and another on isosurfaces and direct volume rendering [Kaufman and Mueller 05] [p.222].
- **Isosurfaces.** Edmond Halley presented isolines in 1686 and contour plots in 1701. Marching Cubes, the standard isosurface algorithm, dates from 1987 [Lorensen and Cline 87], with a survey of the follow-up work [Newman and Yi 06]. Flexible isosurfaces: [Carr et al. 04] [p.222].
- **Direct volume rendering.** The Real-Time Volume Graphics book [Engel et al. 06]; the two foundational 1988 algorithm papers, from Pixar [Drebin et al. 88] and UNC Chapel Hill [Levoy 88]; and the Simian system for multidimensional transfer functions [Kniss 02, Kniss et al. 05] [p.222].
- **Vector fields.** An introductory overview chapter on flow vis [Weiskopf and Erlebacher 05]; state-of-the-art reports on geometric [McLouglin et al. 10], texture-based [Laramee et al. 04] and feature-based [Post et al. 03] flow vis; and LIC as the foundational texture-flow algorithm [Cabral and Leedom 93] [p.223].
- **Tensor fields.** The edited collection Visualization and Processing of Tensor Fields, 25 chapters [Weickert and Hagen 06], including an introduction to diffusion tensor imaging [Vilanova et al. 06] and a comparison of ellipsoid and superquadric tensor glyphs [Kindlmann 04] [p.223].
