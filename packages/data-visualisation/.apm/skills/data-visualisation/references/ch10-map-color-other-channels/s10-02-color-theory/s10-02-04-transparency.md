# 10.2.4 Transparency

## Definition

<!-- verbatim p.250 -->
> A fourth channel strongly related to the other three color channels is transparency: information can be encoded by decreasing the opacity of a mark from fully opaque to completely see-through.
<!-- /verbatim -->

## Rules for use

- Transparency cannot be used independently of the other colour channels: fully transparent marks convey nothing through them [p.250].
- It interacts strongly with luminance and saturation coding and should not be combined with them at all [p.250].
- It can be combined with hue, with a very small number of discriminable steps, most often just two [p.250].
- It is used most often with superimposed layers, to make a foreground layer distinguishable from the background layer [p.250].
- It is frequently used redundantly, encoding the same information as another channel [p.250].
