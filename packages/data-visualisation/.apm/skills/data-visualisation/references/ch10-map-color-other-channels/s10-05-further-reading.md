# 10.5 Further Reading

## Sources by topic

- **All channels in this chapter.** Ware's textbook is an excellent resource for further detail [Ware 13] [p.265].
- **Colour theory.** Stone's brief article [Stone 10] and longer book [Stone 03] are an excellent introduction to colour [p.265].
- **Colormap design.** Segmented colormaps are discussed extensively in cartography; Brewer's very readable guidelines [Brewer 99] go with the ColorBrewer tool at <http://www.colorbrewer2.org>. Early guidelines on quantitative colormaps and the reasons to avoid rainbows are in [Bergman et al. 95, Rogowitz and Treinish 96, Rogowitz and Treinish 98], with more recent work continuing the struggle against rainbows as a default [Borland and Taylor 07]. An empirical study showed bivariate colormaps' serious limits for two categorical attributes [Wainer and Francolini 80] [p.265].
- **Motion.** An empirical study of kinds of motion highlighting confirmed its effectiveness where colour coding already carries other information [Ware and Bobrow 04] [p.266].
- **Texture.** Ware breaks texture into orientation, scale and contrast subchannels, within a thorough discussion of texture for visual encoding [Ware 13, Chapter 6] [p.266].
