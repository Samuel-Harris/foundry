# 13.5 Further Reading

## Sources by topic

- **Filtering.** Early work in dynamic queries popularised filtering with tightly coupled views and extended standard widgets to support these queries [Ahlberg and Shneiderman 94] [p.345].
- **Scented widgets.** Scented widgets [Willett et al. 07] allude to information scent, proposed within the theory of information foraging from Xerox PARC [Pirolli 07] [p.345].
- **Boxplots.** Originally proposed by Tukey and popularised by his book on Exploratory Data Analysis [Tukey 77]; a recent survey covers the many variants proposed over the past 40 years [Wickham and Stryjewski 12] [p.345].
- **Hierarchical aggregation.** A general conceptual framework for analysing it [Elmqvist and Fekete 10]; earlier work presented hierarchical parallel coordinates [Fua et al. 99] [pp.345-346].
- **Spatial aggregation.** The Modifiable Areal Unit Problem in a recent handbook chapter [Wong 09] and in detail in a seminal booklet [Openshaw 84]; geowigs support exploratory analysis that explicitly takes scale into account [Dykes and Brunsdon 07] [p.346].
- **Attribute reduction.** DOSFA [Yang et al. 03a] is one of many approaches from the same group [Peng et al. 04, Yang et al. 04, Yang et al. 03b]; the DimStiller system proposes a general framework [Ingram et al. 10]; an extensive exploration of similarity metrics for dimensional aggregation was influential early work [Ankerst et al. 98] [p.346].
- **Dimensionality reduction.** MDS's foundations were proposed in the 1930s [Young and Householder 38] and developed in the 1950s [Torgerson 52]; an early vis proposal used a stochastic force simulation [Chalmers 96]; Glimmer exploits graphics hardware parallelism for MDS and discusses its history and variants in detail [Ingram et al. 09]; design guidelines for DR data suggest avoiding 3D scatterplots [Sedlmair et al. 13] [p.346].
