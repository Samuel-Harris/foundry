# 15.9 Further Reading

## Sources by system

- **Graph-Theoretic Scagnostics.** Builds on ideas originally proposed by the statisticians John and Paul Tukey [Wilkinson et al. 05, Wilkinson and Wills 08] [p.391].
- **VisDB.** An early proposal of dense displays for multidimensional tables [Keim and Kriegel 94] [p.392].
- **Hierarchical Clustering Explorer.** Its different aspects are described in a series of papers [Seo and Shneiderman 02, Seo and Shneiderman 05] [p.392].
- **PivotGraph.** Visually encodes a different derived data abstraction than most network vis idioms [Wattenberg 06] [p.392].
- **InterRing.** Supports hierarchy exploration through focus+context interaction with geometric distortion and multiple foci [Yang et al. 02] [p.392].
- **Constellation.** Supports browsing a complex multilevel network with a specialised layout and dynamic layering [Munzner et al. 99, Munzner 00] [p.392].
